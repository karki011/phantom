A. Floating Orb

Always-visible glowing orb in bottom-right corner. Click or say "Phantom" to activate. Expands into a chat overlay. Pulses when it has something to say. Lives above all panes.

Pros

  • Instantly accessible from any view
  • Non-intrusive when idle
  • Visual heartbeat (pulse = alive)
  • Siri-like mental model

Cons

  • Overlaps content in small windows
  • Another floating element to manage

B. Command Bar (Spotlight Style)

Press ⌘K or say "Phantom" to summon a floating command bar. Type or speak. Shows contextual suggestions. Dismisses after action. Think macOS Spotlight meets ChatGPT.

Pros

  • Zero screen real-estate when inactive
  • Familiar Spotlight/Alfred mental model
  • Fast keyboard-first workflow
  • Natural for quick questions

Cons

  • No persistent visual presence
  • Multi-turn conversations feel cramped
  • Can't show proactive notifications without interrupting

C. Hybrid — Orb + Expanding Panel (Recommended)

Small status orb in the title bar or rail. Say "Phantom" or press ⌘K for quick commands (Spotlight mode). Click the orb to expand a right-side panel for conversations. Panel persists for multi-turn chat. Orb pulses for proactive notifications.

Pros

  • Quick commands AND deep conversations
  • Always-visible status indicator
  • Proactive notifications via orb pulse
  • Panel doesn't block terminal/editor
  • Familiar: Copilot Chat + Siri combined

Cons

  • More complex to build (two modes)
  • Panel takes horizontal space when open
